## Full description (4000 char max)
Live an entire life — from a baby all the way to age 85! Choose to be a boy
or girl, then grow up through every stage of life in a fully 3D world.

As a baby, rely on your parents — sleep, cry for milk or a diaper change,
and watch the world from your crib. Grow older and head off to school:
choose between two competitive schools (SIP International or Ohlor) or
learn calmly at home with Homeschool.

Study four real subjects — Math, Reading, Science, and Art — each scaled
to your grade level. Watch out for exam days, keep up with homework, and
build friendships with classmates who each have their own personality.

Life throws real surprises your way: relatives dropping by for a visit,
a bully to stand up to (or walk away from, or tell a teacher about),
a missing pet to track down, a wallet you could keep or return, and even
a rare move to a brand new city with a whole new set of friends and
things to learn.

Keep your four stats — Money, Happiness, Health, and Education — balanced
through every choice you make. When you reach age 85, your story starts
over from the very beginning.

FEATURES
- Full life simulation from age 1 to 85 in 3D
- Three different school paths, each with a real tradeoff
- Four real subjects that scale with your grade
- Random life events: bullies, missing pets, found money, relatives
- Multiple cities to live in, each with its own classmates and curriculum
- A cooking mini-game
- Auto-save — pick up right where you left off
