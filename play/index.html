City Life

Live from age 1 to 85 in a modern city

Who are you?

📖 How to Play City Life

🎯 The Goal

Live an entire life — from a baby all the way to age 85! Keep your four stats healthy: 💰 Money, 😊 Happiness, ❤️ Health, and 📚 Education. Reach age 85, or let your Health hit 0, and you start a brand new life.

🕹️ Controls

Arrow keys or WASD to walk. Click on people in the 3D world to interact with them.

👶 Baby Years (Age 1-2)

You're stuck in your crib! Use Cry for Milk / Cry for Diaper when you need something, and Sleep to grow up. Every 100 sleeps = 1 birthday.

🚶 Toddler Years (Age 3-4)

You can walk around now! Use the Cook and Go Shopping buttons for quick mini-games while you wait to start school at age 5.

⏩ Speed

Use the 1x–20x buttons to control how fast days pass.

🏫 Starting School (Age 5)

Your parents ask you to choose:

  • 🏆 SIP International — hardest, but +8 education per study
  • 📖 Ohlor — balanced, +4 education per study
  • 🏠 Homeschool — calm and safe, +5 education, no bullies or exams — but no classmates to make friends with

📚 School Life

Each day has 5 classes — Math, Reading, Science, Art, and PE. Click your teacher to answer a question for your grade — except PE, where you click FAST instead to finish a real challenge like jumping jacks or push-ups! Every 10th day is an Exam Day — fail one and your parents will be furious when you get home! Click your classmates to chat with them.

🚗 Car Rides

You can't go anywhere on your own — Mom or Dad drives you to school (and home again), to the store, and to the hospital. Press Skip if you don't want to watch the whole ride.

🛒 The Grocery Store

Press Go Shopping and you'll arrive in the store's parking lot. Walk out of the car, go through the door, and walk into a 🛒 cart to grab it. Push it around with the arrow keys / WASD. Mom and Dad will put groceries in the cart now and then — you can click the shelves to add things too. The store can have anywhere from 10 to 1000 shoppers (a big crowd slows you down!). When your cart has food in it, push it to a CHECKOUT lane on the left or right side. If you've been getting good grades at school, the cashier gives you a lollipop 🍭!

🌳 Recess

The last part of every school day is recess outside on the sunny grass field. Walk around, click the soccer ball to kick it, and chat with your classmates. Recess ends after a little while, or press Go Home.

🎁 Presents & Toys

Every birthday you get a wrapped present. Click the box to open it! Inside is a toy (or sometimes a card with money). Your toys show up on the purple rug in your room, and the 🧸 Toys button lets you play with them.

❄️ Winter, Santa & Snow Days

Every year ends with winter, and a Christmas tree goes up. On the last day, Santa 🎅 leaves presents under the tree (happy kids get two!). Some years it also snows — and when it snows, there's no school until the new year!

📝 Homework

After a full school day, you'll be assigned homework in a random subject. Do it at home (a button appears) before your next day at school — or the teacher will notice, and you'll lose happiness.

🏙️ Moving Cities

Rarely, your family moves to a brand new city. Your old classmates will miss you, but you'll meet a whole new set of friends — and each city teaches its own special facts too!

🎲 Random Events

  • 😠 Bullies sometimes show up at school — stand up to them, walk away, or tell the teacher (the safest choice!)
  • 🐾 A pet might go missing near your house — find it in time for a reward
  • 💰 You might find a wallet — keep the cash, or turn it in for less money but more happiness
  • 🚪 Relatives visit sometimes — click them to say hi!

🍳 Cooking

Hit the Cook button anytime for a quick cooking mini-game.

💾 Saving

Your game saves automatically. Come back anytime and hit Continue Saved Game!
